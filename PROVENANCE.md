# Provenance

The ICM Minimal Change Engineer was independently designed and newly written in August 2026 around ICM (Intent and Context Management) principles and general, long-established software-engineering practices: minimum viable change, limited and explicit scope, small reviewable diffs, regression testing, separation of concerns, validation distinct from human approval, and source-of-truth artifacts.

No prose, examples, templates, test cases, or document structure were copied from Agency Agents or any other agent repository or framework. Where this package addresses ideas that appear broadly across the software industry (for example, "make the smallest safe change" or "do not delete a failing test to obtain a pass"), those ideas are common professional practice; their expression here — the governing principles, eight-stage workflow, STOP conditions, closed status vocabulary, anti-pattern set, behavioral tests, and all surrounding documentation — is original to this project.

This statement describes the origin of the work in good faith. It is not a legal opinion, and no legal claims are made beyond what is stated above.
